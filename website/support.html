01FAQ

Good to know

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in Field Caliper and tap Reset everything. That clears what the app keeps on this phone: your calibration, units and tool settings. Uninstalling Field Caliper or clearing its storage also removes everything.

Does Field Caliper need the internet?

No. Field Caliper does not request the Internet permission, so it works anywhere, including in flight mode, and it never sends anything off your phone.

Is it free? Are there ads?

It is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create.

Where is my data stored?

Only in the app's private storage on your phone. Nobody else, including us, can see it. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include it in your personal backup, under your control.

Why does it use Vibrate?

For optional haptic feedback when the level reaches true and on taps. You can switch it off in Settings. It gives the app no access to any information.

What is Ruler in Field Caliper?

A yellow ruler runs down the left edge in mm/cm or inches to 1/16. Drag cursors A and B and the span reads out live, for example "42.5 mm · 1 11/16 in · 4.25 cm". Hold a reading, add spans together, and feel a click at every whole centimetre.

What is Protractor in Field Caliper?

A half or full-circle protractor with two draggable arms. The angle shows to 0.1°, with its supplement and reflex angle. Snap to whole degrees when you need to.

What is Level in Field Caliper?

A spirit level from the phone's tilt sensor: a bullseye when the phone lies flat and a tube when it stands on an edge. The bubble turns green within ±0.5° and chimes at 0.0°.

Is it professional advice?

Readings are as accurate as your calibration. For anything critical, check with a physical tool.

Which devices does it run on?

Field Caliper runs on Android 7.0 or newer, on phones and tablets.
